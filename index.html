The Signature Twist

Meet the Power Dice

Every Daily Challenge has one of each Power Dice — and each one plays a role. Use at least 2 of the 3 Power Dice correctly to win. Use all 3 correctly for a Perfect Solve and earn a Puzzle Piece! 🧩

Green — Add or Subtract

The green die must be part of an addition or a subtraction.

Blue — Multiply or Divide

The blue die has to appear in a multiplication or a division.

Red — The Exponent

The red die must be used as an exponent — either the base or the power.
